## How to use this list

This page lists all standard macroeconomic graphs that HWWI produces automatically. The graphs are grouped by topic (GDP, Employment, Prices, Trade) and are regenerated with the latest data at every update.

1. Look through the list or type a keyword into the search box (e.g. "Hamburg", "inflation", "exports") to find a suitable graph.
2. Click **German** or **English** to open the graph with German or English labels.
3. The images can be used directly in presentations, reports, and publications. Please do not edit the graphs; the source is already included in each image.
